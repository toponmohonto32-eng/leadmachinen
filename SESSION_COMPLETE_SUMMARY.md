## 🚀 How to Deploy to Vercel

### Quick Steps (5 minutes):

1. **Go to Vercel**
   - Visit https://vercel.com
   - Sign in with GitHub

2. **Import Repository**
   - Click "Add New" → "Project"
   - Select `toponmohonto32-eng/leadmachinen`
   - Click "Import"

3. **Configure Environment Variables**
   ```
   DATABASE_URL=file:./dev.db
   AI_ZAI_API_KEY=your-api-key-here
   AI_DEFAULT_PROVIDER=zai
   NODE_ENV=production
   ```

4. **Deploy**
   - Click "Deploy"
   - Wait 2-3 minutes
   - Your app is live!

See `VERCEL_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md` for complete step-by-step instructions.
